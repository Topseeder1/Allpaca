Allpaca — A Seedling Remix
==========================

Allpaca runs large language models entirely on your own machine. It is a
Seedling Remix of Ollama — not a reskin, but a bespoke, re-engineered
inference engine built for the Spaces ecosystem.

WHAT IT DOES
------------
  • Runs large language models locally on your hardware, with no cloud
    account required.
  • GPU acceleration for NVIDIA (CUDA), AMD (ROCm) and Vulkan, plus a
    full set of tuned CPU backends.
  • A desktop chat app with conversations, nested projects, an archive,
    and a 30-day Recently Deleted trash you can restore from.
  • Real-time token streaming and a built-in model library manager.

WHAT SPACES IS
--------------
Spaces is Seedling's ecosystem launcher — one window that hosts a whole
family of AI tools, tracks what is installed and running, and routes
their inference through a single local gateway. Allpaca is one of the apps
you launch inside Spaces.

WHAT ARRP IS
------------
ArrP (the ArrP Protocol) is the engine underneath Spaces. It watches your
hardware, decides what can run in parallel and what has to queue, and
streams model output over a local port. Spaces is the UI; ArrP is the
router; Allpaca is the Remix.

A SEEDLING REMIX
----------------
A Remix is Seedling's published rendition of an upstream tool — rebuilt,
hardened and upgraded, the way a coachbuilder reworks a chassis. Allpaca
is not stock Ollama. It is a distinct product, published by Seedling.

Learn more:  https://spacesbyseedling.com/remixes
